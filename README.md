## The entry

> WARNING: Do not let your grandfather test-drive this machine. If he crashes it in 1962, you were never born.

108 characters (limit: 120). `main.ts` writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

- **A real hazard, not a named one.** The grandfather paradox, turned into the joke: the danger isn't the crash, it's that the crash erases the driver.
- **Concrete scene, twist at the end.** A grandfather test-driving the machine in 1962 is a picture you can see; the surprise — "you were never born" — is saved for the last four words.
- **Straight face.** It reads like a genuine safety notice ("WARNING: Do not let…"), so the joke cuts against the official calm.
- **No wasted words.** Every phrase does work; nothing in the line would survive being cut.
